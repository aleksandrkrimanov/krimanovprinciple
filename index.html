The hierarchy

One system of meaning. Four different levels.

The site separates the principle, the paradigm, the platform and the executor so that no application is mistaken for the foundation itself.

01 · PRINCIPLE

The Krimanov Principle

The causal principle: a previous physical action returns as a measurable response, updates the current-state model and determines synthesis of the next interaction.

02 · PARADIGM

Intellectually Defined Interaction with Matter

The general transition from intelligence that controls a predefined actuator to intelligence that determines the physical interaction with matter.

03 · PLATFORM

I-DPM

The technological platform that organizes field diagnosis, response registration, model update, synthesis, physical action and verification in one causally closed loop.

04 · EXECUTION LAYER

Krimanov CyberActuator / K-PEL

The reconfigurable physical execution layer that realizes the synthesized interaction through fields, forces, gradients, energy distributions or combined physical processes.

The causal invariant

The result of one action becomes the physical beginning of the next.

Rn→ Mn→ Wn→ Rn+1
Rn

Measured response of the environment or interaction zone.

Mn

Updated machine-readable model of the current state.

Wn

Synthesized spatial-temporal configuration of physical interaction.

Rn+1

New response after action, used for verification and continuation.
