Press Release 001

Aleksandr Krimanov presents a physical execution platform for intellectually defined interaction with matter

The Krimanov Principle and I-DPM define a model-centered causal architecture through which artificial intelligence can synthesize the next physical interaction from the current measurable response of matter.

LocationTallinn, Estonia
Date25 July 2026
Contacta.krimanov@aaaexpedition.eu

TALLINN, Estonia — 25 July 2026. Independent researcher and inventor Aleksandr Krimanov has presented the Krimanov Principle and the I-DPM platform as a general engineering foundation for the next physical execution level of artificial intelligence.

Current Physical AI systems can perceive the environment, update digital models and adapt commands in real time. Their physical output, however, is commonly executed by mechanisms whose interaction function has already been determined by construction. The announced platform addresses this gap by making the current response of matter a causally necessary input to the synthesis of the next physical interaction.

The previous physical action returns as a measurable response, updates the current-state model and determines synthesis of the next configuration of interaction.
Rn→Mn→Wn→Rn+1

From a principle to an execution platform

The Krimanov Principle defines the causal invariant. Intellectually Defined Interaction with Matter defines the broader paradigm. I-DPM provides the technological platform. The Krimanov CyberActuator, also described as the physical execution layer, realizes the synthesized configuration through reconfigurable physical processes.

Not a software-only proposition

The configuration of interaction is constrained by available energy, conservation laws, dissipation, thermal state, material limits and safety requirements. The execution layer must physically pay for each selected interaction path in real time. The model therefore does not merely choose an abstract pattern; it determines a physically admissible action within the current state of the system and environment.

Implementable with existing components

The platform can be built from existing classes of technology, including field and multiphysical sensors, digital twins and state representations, real-time computing, power electronics, reconfigurable electromagnetic and other physical sources, robotic carriers, and verification systems. The claimed transition lies in their model-centered causal organization rather than in dependence on unknown physical effects.

Applications are branches, not the foundation

Industrial material interaction, autonomous systems for underwater and space environments, intelligent field skin, distributed systems, CyberActuators and Neurointerface 3.0 are implementation branches. The principal public announcement concerns the common platform that makes those branches possible.

Public and patent record

The architecture is connected to international patent application PCT/IB2026/057172, filed on 10 July 2026. The filing is an international patent application, not a granted patent. This website serves as the official public source for the manifesto, terminology and dated announcements; patent documents remain controlling for the legal scope of the application.

Aleksandr Krimanov is an independent researcher, inventor and intellectual property owner based in Tallinn, Estonia. His work concerns model-centered physical execution systems and intellectually defined interaction with matter.
