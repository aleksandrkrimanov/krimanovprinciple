Manifesto of Intellectually Defined Interaction with Matter

The manifesto states the engineering transition from adaptive control of predefined mechanisms to the synthesis of physical interaction itself from the current measurable response and model of matter.

Aleksandr KrimanovTallinn2026

1. The unresolved execution gap

Artificial intelligence can recognize states, build models, predict developments and choose actions. Yet physical execution is still usually delegated to a tool whose physical function has already been fixed by its construction. Intelligence changes the command, while the nature of the interaction remains predetermined.

2. The proposed transition

Intellectually Defined Interaction with Matter introduces a different execution logic. The system measures the response of a causally connected physical environment, updates an operational model of its current state and synthesizes the next configuration of interaction from that state.

The previous action returns to the system as a measurable response and becomes the beginning of the next action.
Rn→Mn→Wn→Rn+1

3. The Krimanov Principle

The Krimanov Principle is the causal invariant of the platform. Response, model, synthesized interaction and renewed response form one continuous loop. Matter is not merely an object of a command; its current physical response is an obligatory source of the next action.

4. I-DPM

I-DPM is the technological platform implementing this principle. Its invariant sequence begins with field or multiphysical diagnosis, continues through response registration and model update, produces a calculated control vector and a reconfigured physical source, performs the target action, and verifies the same zone, flow, trajectory or component.

5. CyberActuator and Physical Execution 3.0

The Krimanov CyberActuator is the physical execution layer of the platform. It does not define the complete architecture by itself. It realizes the configuration calculated by the model through electromagnetic, acoustic, optical, thermal, plasma, mechanical or combined physical processes.

6. Physical admissibility

The synthesized interaction is not a software abstraction. Every configuration must obey conservation laws, available energy, dissipation, thermal limits, material constraints and safety conditions. The execution layer pays the energetic cost of the selected physical path in real time.

7. Realizable today

The platform does not require unknown physics. It can be assembled from existing classes of sensors, digital twins and state representations, real-time computing, power electronics, reconfigurable field sources, sectional or phased structures, robotic carriers and verification systems. The novelty is their causal organization as one model-centered execution architecture.

8. Scope

The same platform can support industrial material interaction, autonomous operation under water, on land and in space, distributed field systems, intelligent field skin and Neurointerface 3.0. These are implementation branches. The announced foundation is the principle and platform common to them.
